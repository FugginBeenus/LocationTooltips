# Location Tooltip

Location Tooltip puts the name of wherever you are standing on your screen, and gives you the tools to decide what those places are called.

At its simplest, you walk around and a small pill at the top of your screen tells you where you are. Cross into a new area and the name changes. That is the whole idea.

Underneath that, there is a region system. You mark out a box in the world, give it a name, and anyone standing inside it sees that name. You can also lock down what people are allowed to do inside that box, so a shop stays unbroken and a spawn area stays peaceful.

![The location pill sitting at the top of the screen](images/hud-overview.png)

## What you get

**A name on screen for wherever you are.** Out in the open it says Wilderness. Inside somewhere you have named, it says that instead.

**Regions you draw yourself.** Two clicks with a wand marks a box. Name it and it is live straight away.

**Protections that stick to a place, not to a player.** Eleven separate switches covering things like block breaking, PvP, explosions and container access. Set them per region, and leave the rest alone.

**Villages that already have names.** Every village in the world gets a place name like Winterdell or Frost Shire without you doing anything. If you run Waystones, the waystone name wins instead.

**Extra pills if you want them.** Coordinates, biome and a clock, each switched on separately and placed wherever you like.
